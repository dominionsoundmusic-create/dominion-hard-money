How We Work With Investors

From the first inquiry to the lender's decision, this is what happens to a deal you send us, and who does what.

Who does what

Two parties handle your deal, and it helps to know which one you are talking to. Dominion Hard Money reviews the deal and prepares it. The third-party lending partner underwrites it, decides, and funds it.

Dominion Hard Money does not lend its own funds. We do not approve loans or set rates. What we do is make sure the lender sees a deal that fits one of its programs, with the information its underwriters need to make a decision without going back and forth.

Have a deal on the table?

Send the address, the numbers and your exit. Our lending partner's team reviews it and tells you whether it fits and on what terms. The lender makes every credit decision.

From inquiry to decision

  1. You send the dealUse the deal inquiry form: the property, purchase price, rehab budget, value after repair, and how you will repay the loan.
  2. We check the fitWe compare it with the lending partner's programs: property type, location, leverage, credit and experience. If it does not fit, you hear why.
  3. You assemble the fileEntity documents, purchase contract, bank statements, scope of work and comparable sales. A complete file is the single biggest thing you control.
  4. The lender underwritesThe lending partner orders the valuation and title work, reviews the borrower and the plan, and decides. If it approves, it sets the terms and you close with the lender.

What the lender looks at

Private lenders underwrite the deal more than the person. Expect questions about four things:

  • The value. What the property is worth today and what it will be worth after the work, supported by recent comparable sales nearby.
  • The budget. A line-item scope of work with contractor bids, broken into phases that match a draw schedule.
  • The borrower. Credit, liquidity for the down payment and reserves, and documented experience over the past three years, which changes leverage and pricing.
  • The exit. How the loan gets repaid: a sale, a refinance into a long-term rental loan, or both as a backup.

The guides on hard money requirements and how hard money financing works go through each in detail.

What it costs to work with us

There are no upfront application fees. Dominion's fee is paid at closing, and only if the loan closes. The lending partner's own costs, interest, points, valuation, title and draw inspections, are set out by the lender in its terms. Our lending partners' short-term programs start at 12.99% interest plus 2.99 points; the lender sets the final pricing for each deal.

Questions about the process

How long does it take to hear back after I send a deal?

We review every inquiry and come back on whether it fits the lending partner's programs. Timing depends on how complete the information is; a deal with the numbers and the exit filled in is the fastest to review.

Who makes the final decision on my loan?

The lending partner. Dominion Hard Money does not lend its own funds and does not approve loans.

What documents should I have ready?

Your LLC or corporation documents and EIN, the purchase contract, recent bank statements, a scope of work with bids, and comparable sales that support your value after repair.

Do I need experience to get a loan through your lending partners?

No. A first-time investor with a well-priced deal, cash for the down payment and reserves, and a realistic budget can be considered. Documented experience usually improves leverage and pricing.
