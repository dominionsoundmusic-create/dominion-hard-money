Private Money Lenders Near Me: Where Local Private Money Really Comes From

"Private money lender" covers two very different things.

Send your deal to our lending partnersSee loan programs

One is a person, a retired landlord or a dentist with savings, lending their own money against your deal. The other is a private lending company with published programs and its own underwriting. Both can be near you, and both can be the right choice. This page shows where to find each one, how a loan from a person should be put on paper to protect you both, and the signs that a "private lender" is neither.

What We Do

Dominion Hard Money connects real estate investors with private lending partners who fund deals that banks will not touch, or will not touch fast enough. We do not lend our own funds. We arrange financing through third-party lending partners who specialize in short-term, asset-based real estate loans.

Our lending partner operates in 37 states and funds loans from $50,000 to $2,000,000. Programs include fix-and-flip loans with rehab draws, bridge loans for time-sensitive acquisitions, DSCR rental loans underwritten on property cash flow, and ground-up construction financing.

Who We Serve

Real estate investors: first-time flippers, experienced rehabbers, landlords building rental portfolios, and developers putting up new construction. The common thread is that every one of them needs capital faster, with fewer restrictions, and with less paperwork than a bank requires.

We work with borrowers at every experience level. A first-time investor with a solid deal and a realiztic budget can get funded. An experienced investor with a track record of completed projects gets better rates and higher leverage.

How It Works

You submit your deal through our loan inquiry form or call us directly. We review the property, the numbers, and your plan. If the deal fits our lending partner's programs, we walk it through underwriting and keep you informed at every step.

There are no upfront application fees. You pay nothing until a loan is approved and you decide to close. The lender handles the appraisal, the title work, and the closing: we handle the communication between you and the underwriting team so nothing falls through the cracks.
