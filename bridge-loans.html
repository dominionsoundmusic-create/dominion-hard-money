What Is a Bridge Loan?

A bridge loan is short-term financing that helps real estate investors move quickly on a property purchase while awaiting the sale of another property or securing permanent financing. Bridge loans are designed for speed — when a deal needs to close fast and traditional bank financing is too slow, a bridge loan is the answer.

At Dominion Hard Money, our bridge loans are asset-based, meaning we lend based primarily on the value of the property — not just your income or credit history. This makes bridge loans ideal for investors who need fast, flexible capital to seize time-sensitive opportunities.
