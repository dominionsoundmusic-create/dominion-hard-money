Fix and flip loans are short-term hard money loans designed specifically for real estate investors who buy distressed properties, renovate them, and sell for a profit. Unlike traditional bank loans, fix and flip loans are approved based primarily on the property value and your exit strategy — not mountains of income documentation.

At Dominion Hard Money, we understand that speed and flexibility are everything in the fix and flip business. Our loan process is streamlined so you can close fast, start your rehab, and get to the finish line without delays.
