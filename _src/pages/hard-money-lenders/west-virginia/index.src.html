Where West Virginia investors are buying

West Virginia's investor activity clusters in a few places with enough sales and renters to support both a flip and a hold.

The Eastern Panhandle: Martinsburg and Berkeley County, and Charles Town and Jefferson County, which sit on the edge of the Washington, DC and Baltimore commuter belt. This is the state's fastest-growing corner, with newer housing and prices closer to the neighboring states.

Charleston and the Kanawha Valley, with South Charleston, St. Albans and Nitro, anchored by state government and health care.

Huntington, part of a metro that crosses into Kentucky and Ohio, with Marshall University in the rental pool.

Morgantown, where West Virginia University drives a large student rental market, and Wheeling in the Northern Panhandle.

Smaller towns in the central and southern coalfields have the lowest prices and the thinnest resale markets. A low purchase price there does not help if nobody will buy the finished house.

Start with the finished value

Our lending partners' minimum loan is $50,000, and the loan cannot exceed 70 percent of the after-repair value. In many states that rules out only a few of the cheapest streets. In West Virginia, with some of the lowest home values in the country, it rules out a real share of small projects, because a house that will be worth $65,000 finished supports a loan of at most about $45,500.

So build the deal from the finished value. Pull recent sales of fully renovated homes on the same street or in the same subdivision. If the finished value cannot support a $50,000 loan, either the scope has to grow or the deal needs a different source of money. The Eastern Panhandle, the better neighborhoods of Charleston and Huntington, and Morgantown clear the bar more easily than older mill and mining towns.

A rental pays double the homeowner's levy

West Virginia assesses property at 60 percent of its value and taxes it by class. An owner-occupied home is Class II. A rental is Class III if it sits outside a municipality and Class IV if it sits inside one, and the levy rates for Classes III and IV are set at double the Class II rate. The seller's bill on a house they lived in will understate yours by about half.

West Virginia property taxes are low by national standards even at the higher class, so this rarely kills a deal. It does change a DSCR ratio, so ask the county assessor for the levy on the correct class before you model the rent.

The deed excise tax. West Virginia charges a state excise tax of $1.10 per $500 of value when a deed is recorded, plus a county excise tax, customarily paid by the seller. On a flip, you pay it when you sell; check the exact county rate with the county clerk.

West Virginia's landlord rules are thin, so the lease does the work

West Virginia never adopted the Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act. Its rules come from a few separate statutes, which means the lease carries more weight here than almost anywhere.

Security deposits: the state does not cap the amount. The deposit, or an itemized statement of deductions, must go back within the shorter of 60 days after the tenancy ends or 45 days after a new tenant moves in. A bad-faith failure to return one can cost more than the deposit itself.

Evictions for unpaid rent go to magistrate court, and state law does not require a separate pay-or-quit notice first, though many landlords give one and the lease may require it.

Leases should spell out rent due dates, late fees, notice periods, entry and repair duties in writing. Where the statutes are silent, the lease is what the magistrate will read.

Foreclosure usually happens outside court through a trustee's sale under a deed of trust, with no right of redemption after the sale.

Can I get a hard money loan in West Virginia?

Yes. Our lending partners review non-owner-occupied investment property in West Virginia. The loan must be at least $50,000 and no more than 70 percent of the after-repair value, so the finished value has to support it, and the lender decides each deal.

Why is the tax on my West Virginia rental higher than the seller's?

An owner-occupied home is Class II. A rental is Class III outside a city or Class IV inside one, and those levy rates are double the Class II rate.

Is a cheap West Virginia house too small for a hard money loan?

It can be. If the after-repair value is under about $71,500, 70 percent of it is under the $50,000 minimum loan. Check recent sales of renovated homes on the same street before you plan a flip.

How long does a West Virginia landlord have to return a security deposit?

Within the shorter of 60 days after the tenancy ends or 45 days after a new tenant moves in, with an itemized statement of any deductions. West Virginia does not cap the size of the deposit.

How are foreclosures handled in West Virginia?

Usually outside court through a trustee's sale under a deed of trust, a faster process than judicial foreclosure, with no redemption period after the sale.
