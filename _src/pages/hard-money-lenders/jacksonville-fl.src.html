A big, spread-out market

Jacksonville is the largest city by land area in the continental United States, because the city and Duval County consolidated into one government in 1968. The wider metro, with Clay, St. Johns and Nassau counties, runs from older urban neighborhoods to beach towns to new suburbs.

That size is part of the opportunity: prices vary a great deal from neighborhood to neighborhood, and the renter base is broad, including military households tied to Naval Air Station Jacksonville and Naval Station Mayport. It is also why the property-level checks matter more here than in a compact city. Two houses at the same price can carry very different insurance bills depending on how close they sit to the river, a creek or the marsh.

Check the water first

Jacksonville sits on the St. Johns River, the Intracoastal Waterway and the Atlantic, with a great deal of low-lying land and tidal creeks in between. A property in a mapped high-risk flood zone will need flood insurance for any lender, and wind coverage is a significant cost across coastal Northeast Florida.

Look up the flood zone before you make an offer, and get actual flood and homeowners quotes during your inspection period. If the house will be insured through Citizens, Florida's insurer of last resort, plan on a flood policy even outside a mapped zone: the Legislature's phased requirement reaches all Citizens personal residential policies with wind coverage by January 1, 2027.

For a rental on a DSCR loan, both premiums go into the payment the rent has to cover, and in some Jacksonville neighborhoods they decide whether the deal works.

Florida landlord rules, flood disclosure and the squatter law

Unpaid rent: a three-day notice to pay or leave, counting business days only, before an eviction filing.

Security deposits: returned within 15 days if the landlord makes no claim, or a written notice of claim sent by certified mail within 30 days.

Flood disclosure to tenants. For leases of one year or longer signed since October 1, 2025, a Florida landlord must hand the tenant a separate flood disclosure covering known flooding, flood insurance claims and federal flood assistance during the landlord's ownership. In a river and coastal city, keep records of every claim.

Squatters. A 2024 Florida law lets a property owner ask the county sheriff to remove unauthorized occupants from a residential property quickly, if the conditions in the statute are met, rather than going through a full eviction. For an investor holding a vacant house during a renovation, that is a meaningful protection. Keep the house secured and visited anyway.

Florida's taxes on the deal

On the loan: documentary stamp tax of 35 cents per $100 on the note and a 0.2 percent intangible tax on the mortgage, usually paid by the borrower. On a $200,000 hard money loan that is about $700 plus $400.

On the deed: 70 cents per $100 of price, customarily paid by the seller outside Miami-Dade. On a $300,000 resale, $2,100.

Property tax: Florida's 10 percent annual limit on assessment increases for non-homestead property resets when the property changes hands, so estimate a rental's tax on what you paid, not on the seller's bill.

Can I get a hard money loan in Jacksonville?

Yes, for non-owner-occupied investment property. Our lending partners' short-term loans start at $50,000 with a 600 minimum credit score, priced from 12.99 percent plus 2.99 points. The lender decides each file.

Do I need flood insurance for a Jacksonville rental?

In a mapped high-risk flood zone, a lender will require it. Outside one, you may still need it if the house is insured through Citizens, whose flood requirement reaches all personal residential policies with wind coverage by January 1, 2027. Get quotes during your inspection period.

How fast can I start an eviction in Florida for unpaid rent?

After a three-day notice to pay or leave that counts only business days, excluding weekends and legal holidays.

How long do I have to return a security deposit in Florida?

Fifteen days if you make no claim on it, or 30 days to send written notice of a claim by certified mail, after which the tenant has 15 days to object.

Can the sheriff remove squatters from my vacant Jacksonville rehab?

Under a 2024 Florida law, an owner can ask the county sheriff to remove unauthorized occupants from a residential property quickly, if the statute's conditions are met, instead of going through a full eviction.

What Florida taxes come with a hard money loan?

Documentary stamp tax of 35 cents per $100 on the note and an intangible tax of 0.2 percent on the mortgage, usually paid by the borrower. On a $200,000 loan, that is about $700 plus $400.
