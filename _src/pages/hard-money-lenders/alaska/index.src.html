In Alaska, most financeable deals are in the Anchorage area

A short-term lender sizes a loan on the after-repair value, and that value has to be supported by recent comparable sales. In most states that is a box to tick. In Alaska it shapes the whole market, because only one metro area has the volume of resales that makes a renovated value easy to prove.

Anchorage itself, from Eagle River to South Anchorage, is the deepest market in the state. Redfin data put the city's median sale price at about $428,000 for the three months ending May 2026, up 3.1 percent on a year earlier.

The Matanuska-Susitna Borough, Wasilla and Palmer, is part of the Anchorage metropolitan area and has been one of the state's busiest places for new housing. Lot sizes vary widely there, and a house on a large rural parcel is harder to value than one in a subdivision.

Everywhere else is harder. The Kenai Peninsula is a few hours' drive from Anchorage, Fairbanks is most of a day, and Juneau is not on the road network at all. Deals there are not ruled out, but thinner sales, remote access and fewer appraisers make them harder to underwrite. Send the address early and the lender will tell you whether it fits.

Valuing a flip without public sale prices

Alaska is a non-disclosure state. Sale prices generally do not appear in the public record, and unlike some non-disclosure states Alaska does not collect them confidentially either, so the public-data tools investors rely on elsewhere are thin here.

Build your after-repair value from closed sales pulled from the multiple listing service by a local agent, and ask about finishes, heating systems and lot size on each comparable. The lender's appraiser works from the same kind of data. In a market this small, a single unusual sale can pull an estimate far off, so look at every comparable yourself.

Costs and seasons that shape an Alaska deal

No transfer tax. Alaska does not tax real estate transfers, which leaves a flip more of its margin.

No statewide property tax. Property tax is set locally. The Municipality of Anchorage levies a different combined rate depending on which police, fire and road service areas an address falls in, and the Mat-Su Borough and its cities set their own. Get the current figure for the exact address before you estimate holding costs.

The building season. Exterior work, foundations and anything that needs the ground thawed is compressed into a short summer. A project bought in the fall with a new roof or siding planned should schedule that work for spring or early summer, choose a loan term that covers the wait, and budget heat for the months the house sits empty.

Materials and labor. Much of what goes into a renovation is shipped in, and good contractors book up fast in summer. Get firm bids with dates before you close.

Foreclosure by trustee sale. Alaska generally forecloses outside court under a deed of trust. The trustee records a notice of default at least 90 days before the sale, and after a nonjudicial sale the former owner has no right to redeem unless the deed of trust grants one. For a buyer at a trustee sale that means a cleaner path to insurable title than in states with a long redemption clock, though a title company should still confirm it.

Alaska's landlord rules

Alaska's Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act applies statewide.

Security deposits are capped at two months' rent, a limit that does not apply when the rent is more than $2,000 a month. They must be returned within 14 days if the tenant gave proper notice to end the tenancy, or 30 days if not or if the landlord is deducting for damage, with an itemized list of deductions.

Unpaid rent: a written notice giving the tenant seven days to pay in full, then an eviction case (forcible entry and detainer) if the tenant neither pays nor leaves.

For a buy-and-hold investor in Anchorage, the rent a unit commands in winter, and the cost of heating it, matter as much as the purchase price. A DSCR loan qualifies on that rent. Can I get a hard money loan in Alaska?

Yes. Our lending partners review Alaska deals, and most of the ones that fit are in the Anchorage area and the Matanuska-Susitna Borough, where there are enough comparable sales to support an after-repair value. The lender decides on each property.

Can I get a fix and flip loan in Fairbanks or Juneau?

It is harder. Thin resale volume, remote access and fewer appraisers make values in Fairbanks, Juneau and the Kenai Peninsula harder to support. Send the address and the numbers first and the lender will say whether it can look at it.

Does Alaska have a real estate transfer tax?

No. Alaska does not charge a transfer tax when real estate is sold, so a flip keeps money that would go to taxes in many other states.

How do I find comps in Alaska if sale prices aren't public?

Alaska is a non-disclosure state, so you cannot reliably look up what nearby homes sold for in public records. An agent with access to the Alaska Multiple Listing Service is the practical way to pull real closed sales for your after-repair value.

Can a former owner redeem an Alaska house after a trustee sale?

Generally no. After a nonjudicial trustee sale under a deed of trust, there is no right of redemption unless the deed of trust itself grants one. Have a title company confirm what it will insure before you bid.

How much security deposit can I charge on an Alaska rental?

No more than two months' rent, unless the monthly rent is more than $2,000. Return it within 14 days if the tenant gave proper notice to end the tenancy, or 30 days if not, with an itemized list of deductions.
