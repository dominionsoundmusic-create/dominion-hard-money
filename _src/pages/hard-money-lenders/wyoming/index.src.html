Wyoming's markets are small, so liquidity matters

Wyoming is the least populated state in the country. Its largest housing markets are Cheyenne, the capital, which sits on the northern edge of the Colorado Front Range about 45 minutes from Fort Collins; Casper; and Laramie, home to the University of Wyoming. Gillette, Rock Springs and Sheridan are smaller and more tied to energy employment, and Jackson is a resort market priced in a world of its own.

In markets this size, a handful of sales sets the comps and a handful of buyers sets the exit. Before you buy, ask how many renovated houses like yours sold nearby in the last six months, and how long they took. That answer drives both the after-repair value and the months of carry in your budget.

Valuing a deal without public sale prices

Wyoming is a non-disclosure state. Sale prices are not generally part of the public record, so automated online estimates are unreliable and you cannot simply look up the neighbors' sales. In a small market, a single unusual sale can pull an estimate far off.

Build your after-repair value from closed sales pulled from the multiple listing service by a local agent, compare finishes and lot sizes yourself, and expect the lender's appraiser to work from the same data.

What helps the numbers in Wyoming

No transfer tax and no state income tax. A flip keeps money that would go to the state in many others, and rental income is not taxed at the state level.

Low property taxes. Residential property is assessed at 9.5 percent of fair market value before local mill levies, which keeps Wyoming's residential property taxes among the lowest in the country. Recent homeowner relief measures apply to an owner's primary residence, so check the county's figure for a rental at the exact address before you estimate holding costs.

Quick landlord remedies. For unpaid rent a Wyoming landlord serves a three-day notice to quit before filing a forcible entry and detainer action, and the process moves quickly. Security deposits go back within 30 days after the rental agreement ends, or 15 days after the landlord receives the tenant's new address, whichever is later, with an itemized list.

The season. Wyoming winters are long, cold and windy. A renovation that includes a roof or siding should plan that work for the warmer months and budget the extra months of carry.

Foreclosure in Wyoming

Wyoming allows foreclosure by advertisement and sale outside court when the mortgage includes a power of sale, which most do. Unlike many nonjudicial states, Wyoming gives the borrower a statutory right to redeem after the sale, generally three months for residential property. An investor buying at a foreclosure sale should expect to wait out that period before the title is clear, and plan the financing and renovation timeline around it.

Can I get a hard money loan in Wyoming?

Our lending partners review non-owner-occupied investment property in Wyoming. Because markets are small and sale prices are private, the lender looks closely at whether there are enough comparable sales to support the value, and decides each deal on its numbers.

Does Wyoming have a real estate transfer tax?

No. Wyoming does not tax real estate transfers, and it has no state income tax, both of which help an investor's numbers.

Is Wyoming a non-disclosure state?

Yes. Sale prices are not generally part of the public record, so an agent with multiple listing service access is the practical way to find comparable sales for an after-repair value.

How does a Wyoming landlord handle unpaid rent?

With a three-day notice to quit before filing a forcible entry and detainer action, which is one of the faster eviction processes in the country.

Is there a redemption period after a Wyoming foreclosure sale?

Yes. Wyoming gives the borrower a statutory right to redeem after the sale, generally three months for residential property, so title from a foreclosure purchase is not clear on the day of the sale.

How long does a Wyoming landlord have to return a security deposit?

Within 30 days after the rental agreement ends, or within 15 days after the landlord receives the tenant's new address, whichever is later, with an itemized list of any deductions.
