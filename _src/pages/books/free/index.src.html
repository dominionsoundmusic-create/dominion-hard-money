The Landlord's Cost Map

What a Rental Really Costs to Hold in Twelve States, 133 pages

The same $400,000 rental run through twelve states: property tax, insurance, and the legal requirements that turn into money. Florida insurance far above the national average. The Small Landlord's Field Guide to the ROAD to Housing Act

What Actually Changed, Where It Changed, and How to Buy in the Gap, 120 pages

From January 7, 2027, entities that control 350 or more single-family homes cannot buy another one. Much of what is circulating about the law describes a version of the bill that never passed. This book sets out what the statute actually says, which markets it changes and which it does not, how to check your own ZIP code, and how to recognize institutional inventory in a listing. It also says plainly when the honest answer is that nothing changes where you live.
