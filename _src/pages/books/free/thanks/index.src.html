Where to start

If you are looking at a specific property, open The Landlord's Cost Map to that state's chapter and work through the checklist at the end of it. If you keep hearing that a new federal law is about to change your market, start with Chapter 1 of the Field Guide.

The download did not open. What should I do?

Tap the link again, or long-press it on a phone and choose to open it in a new tab. The PDFs open in your browser, where you can save a copy.

Can I print the books?

Yes. They are ordinary PDFs, laid out to print at home.
