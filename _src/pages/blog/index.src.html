Latest posts

Looking for a full guide?

The blog covers what changed this week. The guides cover how things work: hard money rates, requirements, fix and flip loans, DSCR loans and the state and city guides.

How often is the blog updated?

A new researched post is published every week, built around one dated figure from a named source.

Where do the numbers in the posts come from?

Each post names its sources, such as ATTOM, Freddie Mac or Apartment List, and the date of the data it uses.

Is the blog financial advice?

No. It explains market data and how investors and lenders commonly use it. Your own deal is priced and decided by the lender on its own facts.
