Which form to use

If you have a property and numbers, the deal inquiry form is faster: it puts the deal in front of our lending partner's team in the shape they need.

For everything else, how the programs work, whether your state is covered, what a deal needs to qualify, a referral or partnership, use the form below.

Send a message

We use your details only to answer your message. See our privacy policy.

Good to know before you ask

  • We are not the lender. Dominion Hard Money does not lend its own funds; it arranges financing through third-party lending partners, who make every credit decision.
  • Investment property only. No loans on a home you will live in.
  • Most answers are already written down. See the FAQ, rates and requirements guides.

How do I check whether my state is covered?

Start with the states page. If your state is not listed there, ask through the form and include the city and property type.

Can I call instead of using the form?

The quickest route is the form, because it gives us the details we need to answer properly. Include a phone number if you would like a call back.

I already sent a deal. How do I follow up?

Choose "Follow up on an existing inquiry" in the form and include the property address, so the message can be matched to your deal.

Do you work with real estate agents and wholesalers?

Choose "Partnership or referral opportunity" and tell us how you work. The borrower still has to meet the lending partner's requirements.
