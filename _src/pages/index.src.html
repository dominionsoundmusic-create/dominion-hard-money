Common Questions

What is a hard money loan?

A hard money loan is a short-term loan secured by an investment property. The lender looks mainly at the property and the deal rather than your personal income. It is usually interest-only and is paid back when you sell the property or refinance it into a long-term loan.

Does Dominion Hard Money lend its own money?

No. Dominion Hard Money does not lend its own funds. We arrange financing through third-party lending partners, and the lending partner is the one that approves and funds the loan.

How much can I borrow, and for how long?

Loan amounts run from $50,000 up to $2,000,000, with terms of 12 to 24 months depending on the program. Leverage goes up to 70 percent of the property's value or up to 100 percent of cost, depending on the program and the deal.

What credit score do I need for a hard money loan?

Most programs start at a 600 credit score, and some ask for more. The DSCR rental loan has no minimum credit score, because it qualifies on the property's rental income instead of your personal income.

Can I use a hard money loan to buy a home I will live in?

No. These loans are for non-owner-occupied investment property only, such as a flip or a rental. The property must be held in an LLC or corporation, not in your personal name.

Are there any upfront fees?

No. There are no upfront application fees. Fees are paid at closing, and only if your loan is approved and you decide to close.

How fast can a hard money deal close?

Often in days rather than the weeks a bank usually takes. The timeline depends on the property, the appraisal and how quickly you send in your documents.
