Who We Are

Dominion Hard Money is based in East Texas and operates nationwide. We do not lend our own funds; we arrange financing through third-party lending partners. We connect real estate investors with private capital solutions, bridge loans, fix and flip loans, and DSCR rental loans, so they can move fast on deals without the delays and restrictions of traditional bank financing.

We are part of the Dominion brand family: a group of companies built to serve entrepreneurs, investors, and business owners with premium services across digital, technology, energy, and financial sectors. Dominion Hard Money was built specifically to serve the real estate investment community with the fast, professional capital access they need to compete and win in today's market.

Our Mission

To give every real estate investor, from first-time flippers to seasoned portfolio builders, access to the private capital they need to close deals fast, grow their portfolios, and build lasting wealth through real estate.
