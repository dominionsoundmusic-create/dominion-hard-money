About Dominion Hard Money

Dominion Hard Money is a Dominion Digital Group brand, based in East Texas, that brings real estate investors to third-party private lending partners.

Who we are

Dominion Hard Money is one of the brands of Dominion Digital Group, the company founded by Maurice Johnson. It is based in East Texas and works with real estate investors nationwide, in the states where its lending partners operate.

The brand exists for one kind of customer: the investor who has found a property and needs short-term, asset-based financing that a bank will not provide, or will not provide in time. That might be a first-time flipper with a well-priced house and a realistic budget, a landlord adding a rental, or an experienced rehabber who needs a lender that understands draws.

What we do, and what we do not do

Dominion Hard Money does not lend its own funds; it arranges financing through third-party lending partners. We are not the lender, and we never make the credit decision. Our role is to take your deal, check it against the lending partner's published programs, tell you what fits and what will not, and introduce a complete file to their team.

  • We do: review your property, numbers and exit; explain the programs in plain English; tell you what documents the lender will ask for; and keep the conversation moving between you and the lender's team.
  • We do not: lend money, approve loans, set rates, or promise a closing date. The lending partner underwrites the property, orders the valuation and title work, decides, and funds.
  • We will not: place a loan on a home you plan to live in. Every program is for non-owner-occupied investment property held in an LLC or corporation.

There are no upfront application fees. Our fee is paid at closing, and only if the loan closes.

Questions about Dominion Hard Money

Is Dominion Hard Money a lender?

No. Dominion Hard Money does not lend its own funds; it arranges financing through third-party lending partners. The lending partner approves, sets the terms of, and funds every loan.

Who owns Dominion Hard Money?

It is a brand of Dominion Digital Group, the company founded by Maurice Johnson, and it is based in East Texas.

Do you charge anything before my loan closes?

No. There are no upfront application fees. Fees are paid at closing, and only if the lender approves the loan and you decide to close.

Can you help with a home I am buying to live in?

No. Every program is for non-owner-occupied investment property such as a flip or a rental, held in an LLC or corporation.

Which states do your lending partners cover?

Most of the country, but not every state. The states page lists the state and city guides and the states where loans are not available.
