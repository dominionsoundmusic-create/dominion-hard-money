Private money for investment property, without the bank's timeline

Dominion Hard Money brings real estate investors to a third-party private lender. We do not lend our own funds. Our job is to understand your deal, match it to the lending partner's programs and get it in front of their team in a form they can decide on quickly.

Hard money is short-term financing secured by the property itself. The lender looks first at what the property is worth now, what it will be worth after the work, and how you plan to pay the loan back, rather than at your pay stubs and tax returns. That is why it suits a distressed purchase, a renovation, or a closing date a bank cannot meet. It costs more than a bank loan, so it works best when the deal has a clear exit: a sale, or a refinance into a long-term rental loan.

Everything here is for non-owner-occupied investment property held in an LLC or corporation. If you plan to live in the house, this is the wrong kind of loan, and we will tell you so.

Questions investors ask before they send a deal

What is a hard money loan?

A short-term loan secured by an investment property. The lender looks mainly at the property and the deal rather than your personal income. It is usually interest-only and is paid back when you sell the property or refinance it into a long-term loan.

Does Dominion Hard Money lend its own money?

No. Dominion Hard Money does not lend its own funds. We arrange financing through third-party lending partners, and the lending partner is the one that approves and funds the loan.

What rate should I expect on a fix and flip or bridge loan?

Our lending partners' short-term programs start at 12.99% interest plus 2.99 points. Borrowers who have paid off two loans with the lending partner in good standing can qualify for pricing from 10.99% plus 1.99 points. The lender sets the final rate for each deal.

What credit score do I need for a hard money loan?

The short-term programs start at a 600 credit score. The DSCR rental loan has no minimum credit score, because it qualifies on the property's rental income instead of your personal income.

Can I use a hard money loan to buy a home I will live in?

No. These loans are for non-owner-occupied investment property only, such as a flip or a rental, and the property must be held in an LLC or corporation rather than your personal name.

Are there any upfront fees?

No. There are no upfront application fees. Fees are paid at closing, and only if the lender approves the loan and you decide to close.
