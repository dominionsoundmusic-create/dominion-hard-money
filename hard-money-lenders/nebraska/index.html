Hard Money Lenders in Nebraska: Financing in Omaha and Lincoln

In Nebraska the landlord process moves quickly, and the property tax bill is often the number that decides a rental.

Omaha and Lincoln are the Nebraska market

Nebraska is a two-metro market for investors. Omaha and Lincoln have the population, the buyers and the renters, landlord rules that move quickly, and closing taxes that are small. The swing number is property tax. This page covers all of it, then our lending partners' terms.

The Omaha metro: Omaha, Bellevue, Papillion, La Vista, Gretna, Elkhorn and Fremont, plus Council Bluffs across the river in Iowa, which runs under Iowa law. The Lincoln metro: Lincoln and the surrounding towns in Lancaster County, with Seward and Crete nearby.

West of Lincoln it gets harder. Grand Island, Hastings, Kearney, North Platte and Scottsbluff are regional centers with smaller markets and fewer comparable sales, and private lenders commonly look harder at that kind of location. Ask about any address west of Lincoln before you commit.

Nebraska's landlord rules move quickly

Nebraska's Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act applies statewide, and a 2021 change (LB 320, approved May 5, 2021) set the seven-day nonpayment timeline landlords use now.

Unpaid rent: a written notice giving the tenant seven days to pay (Neb. Rev. Stat. 76-1431). If the rent is not paid, the landlord can end the rental agreement and file for possession. The trial is set 10 to 14 days after the summons is issued, one of the quicker schedules in the country.

Other serious breaches: a notice giving 14 days to fix the problem, with the tenancy ending at least 30 days after the notice if it is not fixed.

Security deposits: no more than one month's rent, plus a pet deposit of up to a quarter of a month's rent (76-1416). The balance and an itemized list of deductions must go back within 14 days after the tenancy ends.

Month-to-month tenancies end on 30 days' written notice.

What Nebraska adds to the numbers

Documentary stamp tax. Nebraska charges $2.25 per $1,000 of value when a deed is recorded, paid by the seller (76-901). On a $250,000 resale that is about $563, small next to many states.

Property tax. This is the number that deserves the most attention in Nebraska. Rates are set by local districts and are high by national standards, and the state's property tax credit programs, which the Legislature has changed several times in recent years, can reduce the bill. Pull the actual tax statement for the address and the county's current levy, not an estimate, before you set your rent target or size a DSCR loan, because the lender counts the full tax in the payment. Also check whether the seller had a homestead exemption, which is for qualifying owner-occupants only.

Foreclosure. Most Nebraska loans use a deed of trust, and the Nebraska Trust Deeds Act allows the trustee to sell outside court after notice, with no right to redeem once the sale is held. That is a faster route than court foreclosure.

Loans are for non-owner-occupied property held in an LLC or corporation, and the lender decides.

Questions investors ask

Can I get a hard money loan in Omaha or Lincoln?

Yes. Our lending partners review non-owner-occupied investment property in Nebraska held in an LLC or corporation, and most Nebraska deals are in the Omaha and Lincoln metros. The lender decides each deal on its numbers.

Can I get a fix and flip loan in Grand Island or Kearney?

Ask about the exact address. These are smaller markets with fewer comparable sales, and the lender will look closely at the after-repair value and the resale market before deciding.

How much notice does a Nebraska landlord give for unpaid rent?

Seven days' written notice to pay. If the rent is still unpaid, the landlord can end the agreement and file for possession, and the trial is set 10 to 14 days after the summons is issued.

What is the Nebraska documentary stamp tax?

$2.25 for every $1,000 of value when a deed is recorded, paid by the seller. On a $250,000 resale that is about $563.

How much security deposit can a Nebraska landlord charge?

No more than one month's rent, plus a pet deposit of up to a quarter of a month's rent. The balance and an itemized list of deductions must go back within 14 days after the tenancy ends.

Are Nebraska property taxes high for a rental?

They are high by national standards and set locally, and state credit programs change often. Check the actual statement and current levy for the exact address before you estimate holding costs or a DSCR ratio.
