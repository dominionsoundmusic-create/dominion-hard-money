Iowa is the one state where a closing runs without private title insurance.

Where in Iowa deals are easiest to finance

A short-term loan is sized on the after-repair value, and that value needs recent comparable sales behind it. Iowa's larger metros have the deepest resale markets, and they hold most of its population.

Des Moines and its suburbs, West Des Moines, Ankeny, Urbandale, Waukee and Johnston, with Ames about half an hour north.

Cedar Rapids and Iowa City, half an hour apart along the I-380 corridor, with Coralville, Marion and North Liberty between them.

The Quad Cities on the Iowa side: Davenport and Bettendorf.

Council Bluffs, across the river from Omaha and inside its metro.

Where it gets harder: Sioux City, Dubuque, Mason City, Fort Dodge, Waterloo and Cedar Falls are regional centers with smaller resale markets. Deals there are not ruled out, but the value gets closer review. Large rural parcels are harder still. Send the address early and the lender will say whether it fits.

Iowa closes without title insurance

This surprises almost every out-of-state investor. Iowa does not allow private title insurance on Iowa real estate. The state uses a different system, and it changes how a closing works.

The abstract. Each property has an abstract of title, a written history of its ownership and liens, which is updated for each sale.

The attorney's opinion. An Iowa attorney examines the updated abstract and writes an opinion on whether title is good.

The guaranty. Lenders and owners are protected by a title guaranty certificate from Iowa Title Guaranty, a division of the Iowa Finance Authority, rather than by a private policy.

For a hard money borrower the practical effect is time. The abstract has to be located, updated and reviewed before closing, so order it as soon as you have a signed contract and a term sheet. A seller who cannot find the abstract adds cost and delay. Ask about it before you sign.

Iowa's costs and landlord rules

Transfer tax. Iowa charges 80 cents per $500 of value above the first $500, about 0.16 percent. On a $250,000 resale that is roughly $400.

Landlord law. Iowa's landlord and tenant act applies statewide. A security deposit can be no more than two months' rent and must be returned within 30 days after the tenancy ends and the tenant gives a forwarding address, with an itemized statement. For unpaid rent the landlord can serve a three-day notice to pay before ending the tenancy and filing for eviction.

City rental rules. Several Iowa cities, Des Moines among them, run their own rental registration and inspection programs. Check the city's rules before you buy a rental, since an inspection can come with a list of required repairs.

Property tax. Iowa taxes residential property on a rolled-back share of assessed value set by the state each year, and the homestead credit is for owner-occupants only. Budget a rental's tax from the county assessor's figures without the credit, not from the seller's bill.

Foreclosure goes through the courts in Iowa, and depending on the mortgage and the type of foreclosure, the former owner can have a period after the sheriff's sale to redeem the property, up to a year in some cases. Before you bid at a sheriff's sale, ask an Iowa attorney whether a redemption period applies and how long it runs, because the lender will need clear title to fund.

Questions investors ask

Can I get a hard money loan in Iowa?

Yes. Our lending partners review Iowa deals, and most that fit are in Des Moines, Cedar Rapids and Iowa City, the Quad Cities and Council Bluffs. The property must be a non-owner-occupied investment held in an entity, and the lender decides on each one.

Why is there no title insurance on my Iowa closing?

Iowa does not allow private title insurance on Iowa property. Title is checked through an abstract reviewed by an attorney, and lenders are protected by a certificate from Iowa Title Guaranty, a division of the Iowa Finance Authority. Order the abstract update as soon as the contract is signed.

How much is the Iowa transfer tax on a flip?

Eighty cents for every $500 of value above the first $500, about 0.16 percent. On a $250,000 sale that is roughly $400.

How much security deposit can I charge on an Iowa rental?

No more than two months' rent. Return it within 30 days after the tenancy ends and the tenant provides a mailing address, with an itemized statement of anything withheld.

How fast can I evict for unpaid rent in Iowa?

The landlord gives a three-day written notice to pay. If the rent is not paid within those three days, the landlord can end the rental agreement and file for eviction.

Can I finance a flip in Sioux City or Dubuque?

Possibly, but check the exact address first. Smaller resale markets make after-repair values harder to support, so those deals get closer review. Send the property and numbers through the form for an answer.
