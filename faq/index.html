Loan Programs

What is a hard money loan?

A short-term, asset-based loan secured by an investment property. The lender evaluates the property and the deal rather than the borrower's income. Typical terms are 6 to 12 months, interest-only, repaid by selling the property or refinancing into a long-term loan.

What types of loans does Dominion Hard Money offer?

Fix-and-flip loans (purchase plus rehab), bridge loans (short-term gap financing), DSCR rental loans (long-term based on rental income), cash-out refinance, and construction loans. All arranged through our third-party lending partner.

Does Dominion Hard Money lend its own funds?

No. Dominion Hard Money does not lend its own funds. We arrange financing through third-party lending partners who specialize in private money real estate loans.

What is a DSCR loan?

A DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) loan is a long-term rental loan underwritten on the property's rental income rather than the borrower's personal income. The ratio of monthly rent to monthly payment determines qualification. No W-2s, no tax returns, no DTI calculation. Full DSCR guide →

Requirements & Eligibility

What are the minimum requirements?

Minimum credit score of 600-650 depending on the program. Non-owner-occupied investment property held in a business entity (LLC, corporation, or trust). A clear exit strategy. Property within reasonable distance of a metro area of at least 200,000 people.

Can I use a hard money loan for a primary residence?

No. All loans arranged through Dominion Hard Money are for non-owner-occupied investment properties only. If you plan to live in the property, hard money is not an option.

Do I need an LLC?

Most programs require the borrower to be a business entity — LLC, corporation, or trust. If you do not have one, you can form one before closing. Some states allow formation in a few days.

What states do you serve?

Our lending partner operates in 37 states. See the full list. Some states have licensing requirements that limit certain loan types.

Costs & Fees

How much does a hard money loan cost?

Interest rates run 10 to 14 percent for short-term loans with 2 to 4 origination points. DSCR rental loans start lower, around 6 to 8 percent. Total cost depends on the property, the LTV, and the borrower's experience. Use our hard money calculator to estimate.

Are there upfront fees?

No. There are no upfront application fees. You pay nothing until a loan is approved and you decide to close. Origination points and fees are collected at the closing table.

Process & Timeline

How fast can a hard money loan close?

Most close in 7 to 21 days. The timeline depends on the property, the appraisal, and how quickly you provide documentation.

How do rehab draws work?

The lender holds the rehab budget in escrow and releases it in draws as work is completed. After finishing a phase, you request a draw. The lender inspects and releases funds within 3 to 5 business days. Most loans include 4 to 6 draws. Full rehab loan guide →

What happens after I submit a loan inquiry?

We review your deal within 24 hours and respond with next steps. If the deal fits our programs, we walk it through underwriting and keep you informed at every step. If it does not fit, we tell you why and what would need to change.
