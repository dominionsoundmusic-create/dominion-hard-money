1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to website owners about how visitors use their site.

Cookies do not contain personally identifiable information on their own, but they can be linked to personal information that you have provided to us or that we have collected from other sources.

2. How We Use Cookies

Dominion Hard Money uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on dominionhardmoney.com for the following purposes:

  • To ensure the website functions correctly and reliably
  • To remember your preferences and settings during your visit
  • To analyze how visitors use our website so we can improve it
  • To understand which pages and content are most useful to our visitors
  • To measure the effectiveness of our marketing efforts

5. How to Control Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways. Most web browsers allow you to manage your cookie preferences through the browser settings. You can typically:

  • View cookies that have been set on your device
  • Allow, block, or delete cookies from specific websites
  • Block all cookies from all websites
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser

Please note that blocking or deleting cookies may affect the functionality of our website and other websites you visit. Some features may not work correctly if cookies are disabled.

To manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome — Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox — Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari — Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge — Settings > Privacy, search, and services > Cookies

6. Do Not Track

Some browsers include a Do Not Track feature that signals to websites that you do not want your online activity tracked. Our website does not currently respond to Do Not Track signals. We will update this policy if our practices change.
