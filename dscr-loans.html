What Is a DSCR Loan?

DSCR stands for Debt Service Coverage Ratio. It is a measure of how much income a rental property generates compared to its debt obligations. A DSCR loan qualifies borrowers based on the property's rental income rather than the borrower's personal income — making it ideal for self-employed investors, retirees, and anyone who doesn't fit the traditional W2 borrower mold.

For example, if a rental property generates $2,000 per month in rent and the monthly debt payment is $1,500, the DSCR is 1.33 — meaning the property earns 33% more than it costs to carry. This is the kind of analysis we use to approve DSCR loans — not your pay stubs or tax returns.
